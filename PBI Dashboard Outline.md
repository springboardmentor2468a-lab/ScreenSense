**Slide 1 — Title \& Introduction**

Title: "ScreenSense: Kids' Screen Time Analysis — India 2025"

Dataset: Indian Kids Screentime 2025, Kaggle | 9,712 records | Ages 8–18

Analyzed how much time Indian children spend on screens, what devices they use, and what health impacts result



**Slide 2 — The Problem (Why this matters)**

Question: "How many hours a day do you think kids spend on screens?"

WHO recommendation: 1–2 hours/day for children

Our finding: average is 4.35 hours — more than double

This sets up why the dashboard exists

**Slide 13 — Recommendations**

For Parents: Set phone screen time limits at age 11, before habits form

For Educators: Address Poor Sleep impact on learning in health classes

For Policymakers: Distinguish educational vs recreational screen time in national guidelines
