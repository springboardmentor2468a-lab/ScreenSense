# ScreenSense: Kids’ Screen Time Visualization Dashboard

## Project Objective
Analyze children’s screen time behavior to identify patterns across **age, gender, device usage, location type, and activity purpose**, and present insights through an **interactive Power BI dashboard** for parents, educators, and policymakers.

---

# Dashboard Structure

**Total Pages:** 4

1. Executive Overview  
2. Demographic Insights  
3. Device & Usage Behavior  
4. Health & Risk Impact  

Each page answers a specific analytical question.

---

# Page 1: Executive Overview

## Purpose
Provide a quick **high-level summary** of the dataset and highlight key screen time patterns.

## KPI Cards
- Total Children in Dataset
- Average Daily Screen Time
- Percentage of Kids Exceeding Recommended Screen Time
- High Engagement Risk Users
- Kids Experiencing Health Impacts

## Visualizations

### Scatter Plot
**Age vs Average Daily Screen Time**  
Shows how screen usage varies across different ages.

### Line Chart
**Average Screen Time by Age Band**  
Displays screen time trend across age groups.

### Bar Chart
**Average Screen Time by Device Type**  
Identifies which device contributes most to screen usage.

### Treemap
**Device Usage Distribution**  
Shows how device usage is distributed among children.

### Donut Chart
**Screen Time Level Distribution**  
Shows proportion of children in Low, Moderate, and High screen time categories.

---

# Page 2: Demographic Insights

## Purpose
Understand how **demographic factors influence screen time behavior**.

## Visualizations

### Clustered Column Chart
**Average Screen Time by Gender**  
Compares screen usage between boys and girls.

### Stacked Bar Chart
**Urban vs Rural Distribution by Gender**  
Shows demographic distribution across locations.

### Line Chart
**Average Screen Time by Age**  
Identifies age-wise screen time progression.

### Stacked Column Chart
**Age Band vs Engagement Risk Level**  
Highlights which age groups face higher engagement risks.

### Donut Chart
**Gender Distribution of Children**  
Provides overall gender composition of the dataset.

---

# Page 3: Device & Usage Behavior

## Purpose
Analyze how children **access devices and the purpose of screen usage**.

## Visualizations

### Horizontal Bar Chart
**Average Screen Time by Primary Device**  
Identifies the most time-consuming device.

### Donut Chart
**Device Access Type Distribution**  
Shows how kids access devices (shared, personal, etc.).

### Matrix / Heatmap
**Device Type vs Usage Purpose**  
Shows relationship between device types and activity purposes.

### Scatter Plot
**Educational vs Recreational Ratio vs Screen Time**  
Analyzes whether educational usage reduces overall screen exposure.

### Stacked Bar Chart
**Usage Purpose by Device Type**  
Shows which devices are used more for education vs recreation.

---

# Page 4: Health & Risk Impact

## Purpose
Identify potential **health and engagement risks related to screen time**.

## KPI Cards
- Kids with Health Impacts
- High Engagement Risk Users
- Percentage Exceeding Screen Limit

## Visualizations

### Donut Chart
**Health Impact Distribution**  
Displays common health issues linked with screen time.

### Clustered Column Chart
**Screen Time Level vs Health Impact**  
Shows how health issues increase with screen time.

### Funnel Chart
**Engagement Risk Level Distribution**  
Highlights proportion of low, medium, and high risk users.

### 100% Stacked Column Chart
**Screen Time Level vs Engagement Risk**  
Shows correlation between screen time intensity and engagement risk.

### Bar Chart
**Average Screen Time by Health Impact Category**  
Analyzes which health issues are linked to higher screen time.
